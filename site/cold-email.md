# Cold-email templates — selling the Lead Engine (done-for-you)

**Pitch site to link:** `https://whimsical-heliotrope-a56c01.netlify.app`  (host `site/index.html` — see README.md)

The ask is a **reply by email**, not a booked call. Lower friction: they just hit reply.

Rules these follow (same as the engine's own drafts): open on a *specific* detail about them,
connect it to one concrete outcome, end with one low-friction question. No "I hope this finds
you well", no feature lists, no greeting/signature fluff in the body.

Replace `{{...}}` before sending.

---
